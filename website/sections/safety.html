Safety by design

Clean without fear.

Most cleaners ask you to trust them. Moleui is built so you do not have to: it previews first, deletes to the Trash, refuses to touch protected paths, and logs everything it does.

When uncertainty exists, Moleui refuses, skips, or asks, rather than widening what it deletes.

Preview first

Every module runs a dry scan and shows you the full list before anything changes. You approve, it acts.

Trash, not oblivion

Deletions route to the macOS Trash instead of permanent removal. Change your mind? Put it back.

Protected paths

System roots, keychains, credentials, browser history, and even your AI tools are off limits, always, even with admin rights.

Everything on the record

File-removing operations are logged locally to an operations log you can read any time.

Open source under MIT. Every line that touches your files is public on GitHub.
